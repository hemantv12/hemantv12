Privacy Notice

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Collabzy helps creators and brands discover and manage collaboration opportunities. This notice explains the information used by the current early-access version.

Information we collect

When you register, we store your name, email address, password hash, account type and the profile details you choose to provide. We also store campaigns, pitches, proposed rates and application statuses that you submit.

How we use it

We use account and campaign information to provide login, creator profiles, campaign discovery and application management. Passwords are stored as hashes, not as readable passwords.

Instagram

Instagram insights are not currently connected in this build. We will only access Instagram data after an official Meta authorization flow and the required permissions are configured. Never share your Instagram password with Collabzy.

Payments

Paid membership checkout is not enabled in this build. Collabzy does not collect membership payments through this version.

Storage and early access

This is an early-access service. Data storage and backup arrangements are still being finalized; do not use it as the sole record of important commercial agreements or sensitive information.

Your choices

You can choose what profile information to provide. For account access or deletion requests, contact support@collabzy.app.

Changes

We may update this notice as features are added. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
