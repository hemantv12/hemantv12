Privacy Notice

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Collabzy helps creators and brands discover, invite, apply for and manage collaboration opportunities. This notice describes the current early-access version.

Information we store

Account details include name, email, a password hash and account type. Profile details can include niche, bio, location, Instagram handle, follower count and engagement rate. We store campaigns, invitations, application pitches, proposed rates, statuses, conversations, messages, saved campaigns and notifications.

How information is used and shared

Information is used to authenticate accounts and operate the marketplace. Creator profiles are visible to signed-in users of the creator directory. Campaign listings are visible in the marketplace; applications and related conversations are shared with the creator and brand participating in that collaboration. Do not include confidential information in public profile fields or campaign listings.

Instagram

Collabzy has an official Meta Instagram Login integration, but it is not active until the Meta app credentials, redirect URI and required permissions are configured. If you authorize the connection, the app can process the permitted professional-account profile, media and insights returned by Meta. Access tokens are encrypted at rest by the server. Collabzy does not ask for or store your Instagram password and does not scrape private Instagram data. You may disconnect Instagram in the app; this removes the stored token.

Security

Passwords are hashed. We use authentication tokens and server-side access checks for account data. No internet service is risk-free; avoid sending sensitive contractual, financial or personal information through early-access messaging.

Payments

Membership checkout is not enabled in this build. Collabzy does not collect membership payments through this version.

Storage and early access

This deployment still uses SQLite on a free hosting service. Its local filesystem is ephemeral and records may be lost when the service restarts, spins down or redeploys. Persistent production storage and backups have not yet been verified. Do not use this version as the sole record of commercial agreements or sensitive information.

Your choices

You can update your profile, change your password, download a copy of your account data, disconnect Instagram, or request account deletion from the Profile area. Account deletion removes the account and related marketplace records and may remove associated conversations. For data access and deletion requests, use the Profile controls in Collabzy. A direct privacy contact address must be published before public launch.

Changes

This notice may be updated as the product changes. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
