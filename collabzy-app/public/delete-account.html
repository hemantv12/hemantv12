Delete your Collabzy account

You can delete your account and associated marketplace data yourself from the web app. This deletion page is available even when the Android app is not installed.

Delete an account you can access

  1. Open the web app with the button below.
  2. Sign in with the account you want to delete. If needed, use the account's password reset link first.
  3. Open Profile, scroll to Delete account, choose the delete option and confirm using the account password.
Sign in and open Profile →

What gets removed?

Deleting your account removes the user record and its associated profile, owned campaigns, applications, saved campaigns, invitations, conversations, messages, notifications and email action tokens through database relationships. Messages or campaign records may also be removed for other users where they are linked to the deleted account.

Can't sign in?

Use Support and choose “Account deletion”. Include the account email and a description of the access problem. For safety, an inaccessible account is not deleted solely from an unverified email request; the operator must verify ownership before acting.

If the support channel is not configured, the owner must complete that setup before accepting public deletion requests outside the in-app self-service flow. Privacy and Terms pages explain the current implementation.
