## 2-week starter cadence
- Week 1: Studio introduction; service overview carousel; short creative process reel.
- Week 2: Brand/website teardown tip; behind-the-scenes workflow; a practical campaign checklist.
- Adapt 9:16 video to Reels/Shorts, a square or 4:5 post to Instagram/Facebook, and a text-led insight to LinkedIn/X. Keep the core message consistent but rewrite captions for each audience.

## Launch and connection checklist
1. Check for an existing account/page before creating another.
2. Create accounts using an owner-controlled email/phone and finish verification directly with each platform.
3. Turn on two-factor authentication and store recovery codes in the owner's password manager.
4. Create the Facebook Page and Instagram professional profile under the business owner's Meta Business portfolio.
5. Create LinkedIn Company Page from a real owner's existing personal LinkedIn profile; do not create a fake personal profile for the business.
6. Create the YouTube channel under the owner's Google account/Brand Account.
7. Connect Instagram/Facebook via Meta Business Suite and only grant the permissions needed.
8. Connect Metricool for scheduling/analytics after the owner approves the integration. Consider Windsor.ai later if a unified advertising/analytics view is needed.
9. Add profile links, the verified business contact channel, and a verified address only where appropriate.
10. Test links, profile previews and post formats before publishing.

## Credentials safety
Do not put passwords, OTPs, recovery codes or API keys in the website, repository, Canva document, or ChatGPT conversation. The owner should create and retain the primary login, then use a password manager's secure share feature if an employee needs delegated access. Prefer platform roles (Meta Page access, LinkedIn Page admin, YouTube channel permissions) over sharing a personal password.
