BRAND IDENTITY & DESIGN / SERVICE 03

Be recognised before you are read.

Distinctive brands need more than a logo. We develop a visual language—type, colour, composition and campaign assets—that feels coherent across social, print, presentations and digital products.

The objective

Brand & graphic design.
With purpose.

Brand identity, logo exploration, typography, colour systems, campaign graphics, social assets and presentation design.

We start by agreeing audience, objective, deliverables and review process. Then creative direction and production are shaped around the brief, not a one-size-fits-all package.

  • A clearer, more ownable visual identity
  • Consistent assets across channels
  • Reusable design rules instead of one-off graphics
  • A foundation for campaigns and digital work
Potential deliverables

What this can
include.

  • Visual direction and moodboards
  • Logo and identity explorations
  • Typography, colour and layout system
  • Social media graphics and campaign key visuals
  • Pitch decks and marketing collateral
  • Usage guidelines for consistent execution

Exact deliverables, timeline, review rounds, account access and pricing are confirmed in the project scope before work begins.

Questions people ask

Before we
get started.

Clear expectations make better partnerships. Here are a couple of common questions about this service.

Can you refresh an existing logo?

Yes. We can either refine an existing identity or explore a wider rebrand depending on the brief.

Will we own the final files?

Final formats, source files and usage rights are set out clearly in the agreed project scope.

We do not promise rankings, virality, follower growth, leads or revenue outcomes. Targets and measurement plans are agreed for the actual project and evaluated using real data.
