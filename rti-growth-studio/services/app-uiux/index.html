Questions people ask

Before we
get started.

Clear expectations make better partnerships. Here are a couple of common questions about this service.

Can you design an app before development starts?

Yes. Prototypes and key flows can help validate the experience before full development.

Does UI/UX include coding?

UI/UX focuses on structure, usability and interface design. Development can be scoped separately or alongside it.

We do not promise rankings, virality, follower growth, leads or revenue outcomes. Targets and measurement plans are agreed for the actual project and evaluated using real data.
