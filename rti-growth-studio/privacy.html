Privacy Policy.

How AA Growth Studio handles submitted project enquiries, uploaded media, technical storage and external services.

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This page describes the current preview website. Before a production launch, confirm the final business contact details, hosting configuration, analytics tools and any third-party services used.

1. Information you enter

The current project-enquiry form runs in your browser and generates a text brief for you to copy. It does not submit the fields to an AA Growth Studio server, send an email automatically or save the brief in an online account. You choose whether and where to share the copied brief.

2. Website technical information

The site uses local HTML, CSS, SVG and JavaScript assets. The first-visit animation stores a simple session flag in your browser's session storage so that the introduction does not replay every time you move between pages in the same tab. That flag is not used to identify you.

The website hosting provider may process standard technical request logs as part of serving the site and protecting its infrastructure. The precise retention and fields depend on the hosting provider's current settings and policies.

3. External links

Some links may open third-party sites such as Google Maps. If you follow those links, the third party's privacy terms and practices apply to that visit.

4. Cookies and analytics

This version does not intentionally include an advertising pixel or third-party analytics script. If analytics, tracking pixels, embedded forms, chat widgets or other data collection tools are added later, this notice should be updated and any required consent or disclosures provided before those tools are activated.

5. How to contact the studio

For a privacy question, use the contact page to prepare a brief and send it through a channel agreed directly with the studio. An email address or server-side form will be listed here once a verified contact channel is configured.

6. Updates to this notice

This notice may be updated when the website, the tools used on it or the enquiry process changes. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
