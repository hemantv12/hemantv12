SUBSATHI BUSINESS MODEL

Help users save money.
Build a profitable business.

Keep useful subscription tracking free, charge for valuable advanced tools, and earn transparently from relevant partner referrals. Never sell inbox data or recommend a plan just for commission.

PRIMARY REVENUEPremium plans

Charge for multiple inboxes, advanced alerts, family dashboards and detailed savings reports.

SECONDARY REVENUEReferral commission

Earn only when a user voluntarily visits a clearly disclosed partner offer and completes an eligible action.

LATER STAGEFamily / power users

Offer shared household dashboards and multi-account automation once core detection works reliably.

Product principle

A production SubSathi should use explicit Google OAuth consent, request only the minimum email access required, explain what is scanned, avoid storing full email contents when possible, protect saved subscription metadata, and provide disconnect and delete controls.
