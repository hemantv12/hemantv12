Privacy notice

Updated 13 October 2026

Current beta behaviour: this web workspace stores subscription entries in your browser on this device. Receipt files and pasted receipt text are parsed locally. Gmail, bank, SMS, payments and cross-device account sync are not currently connected.

Information handled by this version

Subscription names, amounts, categories, billing cycles, renewal dates, notes and management links you enter are saved in browser local storage when available. The receipt importer analyses only the files or text you select and submit in this page. Do not paste passwords, full card numbers, bank credentials or other secrets.

Where data is stored

The browser workspace is device-specific and does not currently synchronise records to a SubSathi account. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to access locally saved entries. Hosting providers may process normal technical request data to serve this website.

Backups, export and deletion

Use Data tools → Download backup to create a JSON copy, and Restore backup to merge a saved copy into this browser. Use Settings to export CSV or clear the local records. Clearing browser/site data may remove records permanently, so keep a backup if you need one.

Third-party services and tracking

This version does not connect to Gmail, bank accounts or SMS, and does not process payments. Subscription-management links open the provider website you choose. A production release should publish its final analytics, payment, Google OAuth and retention practices before enabling those integrations.

Questions and requests

Before enabling account connections or accepting sensitive user data, the SubSathi operator must publish a monitored privacy contact and replace this beta notice with a reviewed policy that names the responsible legal entity, retention periods and applicable rights.
