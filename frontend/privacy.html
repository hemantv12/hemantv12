Privacy notice

Updated 13 October 2026

Beta status: the web workspace stores entries in this browser. The Android app stores subscription tracker entries on the device. Gmail discovery and live ad revenue require owner-managed service configuration and review; this test build may display Google test ads only.

Information you enter

Subscription names, amounts, categories, billing cycles, renewal dates, notes and management links are saved in browser local storage in the web workspace, or in local SQLite storage in the Android app. These local records do not automatically sync between your devices or to a SubSathi account. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to see them.

The web receipt importer reads only the .eml/.txt files or text you select and processes the content in your browser. Do not enter passwords, full card numbers, bank credentials or other secrets.

Optional Gmail connection

Gmail discovery is unavailable until the operator configures Google OAuth, enables the Gmail API and completes any required Google review. If enabled, you must approve Google’s consent screen. SubSathi is designed to request read-only Gmail access and search for billing-related messages; it never asks for your Gmail password. The backend parses message content to identify subscription evidence and does not retain full message bodies as part of the scan. It may retain extracted metadata such as service name, amount, billing cycle, renewal date, evidence date and confidence so you can review detections. Google OAuth credentials are intended to be encrypted on the server.

When Gmail is connected, use Disconnect Gmail to request revocation and remove the server-stored credential copy and cached scan candidates. Use Delete SubSathi server account to request removal of server-side account data. Imported tracker entries saved locally on your phone must be deleted separately. These server features depend on the API and persistent database being configured correctly.

Optional AI-assisted Gmail extraction

AI-assisted extraction is optional and off by default. If the operator has configured an AI API key and you explicitly enable the AI-assisted option before a scan, SubSathi may send up to 25 incomplete or uncertain billing-related email excerpts per linked inbox, with a maximum of 3,500 characters of message body per excerpt, to OpenAI's API to identify subscription names and evidence for prices, billing cycles and renewal dates. Messages already fully identified by the local parser are not sent for AI analysis. The model is instructed to return exact evidence quotes; SubSathi validates those quotes against the supplied text and only fills fields supported by the email. AI results can still be wrong and must be checked with the provider.

Do not enable AI-assisted extraction unless you agree to this additional processing. Email excerpts are sent to the external AI provider for processing and are not stored as full email bodies in SubSathi's scan database. Handling and retention by the AI provider are subject to that provider's API terms and policies. If AI is not configured, the option remains unavailable and standard local-rule extraction continues.

Advertising in the Android app

The Android beta integrates Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform for consent handling. The currently configured build uses Google’s test App ID and a test banner placement; test advertisements do not generate earnings. Before real ads are enabled, the operator must configure its own AdMob app and ad-unit IDs, publish appropriate disclosures and complete Google’s app-readiness review.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share information such as IP address, ad/product interactions, diagnostics and device or advertising identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud-prevention purposes. Ad requests are configured for non-personalized ads in this app, but this does not mean the SDK processes no information. Review Google’s Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy for details. Where Google requires a consent form, SubSathi asks Google’s consent SDK to present it before initializing ads.

Hosting and third-party services

Hosting providers process technical request data to serve the site and API. Gmail authorization, where enabled, is handled by Google; advertising requests, where enabled, are handled by Google Mobile Ads. Subscription-management links open the provider website you choose. No bank or UPI account is currently connected, and SubSathi does not process subscription payments.

Backups, export and deletion

In the web workspace, use Data tools → Download backup to create a JSON copy and Restore backup to merge a saved copy into this browser. Settings includes CSV export and local data clearing. In the Android app, clear tracker data from Settings to remove records on that device. Clearing browser or app data may permanently remove local records, so keep a backup if you need one.

Retention and contact

Local records remain on your device until you delete them or the app/browser data is removed. Server-side data retention depends on the production database and configured account deletion flows. Before a public production launch, the operator must publish a monitored privacy contact, responsible legal entity, retention periods and applicable user rights. Do not treat this beta notice as a completed legal review.
