### Instagram caption
Facebook lead ads can make it easy for someone to send their details. The harder part is helping your team recognize which requests deserve a real follow-up.

For a US small business running Meta lead forms, use this quick audit:
1. Make the offer specific enough that the right person recognizes themselves.
2. Ask one useful qualifier that changes routing or the next conversation—such as project type, service area, or timing.
3. Explain what happens after submit: who follows up, through which channel, and what to expect.
4. Track the path beyond the form: contacted, qualified, booked, and closed (or not a fit).

Don't add questions just to make the form longer. Every field should help someone decide, qualify, or follow up.
