## Campaign
**Title:** The Milestone Isn't the Insight.
**Buyer:** US SaaS and startup founders building founder-led visibility.
**Issue:** Founder updates often report milestones without revealing the thinking that makes the work useful to peers and potential buyers.
**Primary buyer-intent search phrase:** founder-led marketing
**Supporting phrases:** founder content strategy; SaaS founder marketing; B2B thought leadership; startup content marketing.

## Instagram caption
Founder-led marketing doesn’t need another milestone announcement. It needs a point of view.

A milestone tells people what happened. A decision note shows how you think.

Try this three-part structure:
• Context — What constraint or customer need mattered?
• Choice — What did you decide, and what did you deliberately leave out?
• Learning — What would you repeat or rethink?

Keep private customer details, confidential numbers and roadmap information out of it. Share the reasoning, not sensitive information.

What’s one decision your team made recently that another founder could learn from?
