# Future Reel Script — A Tour Isn't a Win

**Length:** 20–24 seconds  
**Format:** Text-led, real tabletop/whiteboard footage or a genuine, permissioned product recording. No synthetic customer footage, fake product screens or AI people.

| Time | Shot | On-screen copy / voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Close-up of a blank onboarding map being sketched on paper. | “A product tour isn't the same as a first win.” |
| 3–7s | Write three words in sequence: SIGN UP → ONE TASK → FIRST VALUE. | “Your first session should lead somewhere useful.” |
| 7–12s | Cross out optional steps that do not help the first task. | “Start with the job they came to do. Remove the detours.” |
| 12–17s | Circle the one meaningful action and mark the outcome. | “Make the payoff visible before introducing more features.” |
| 17–22s | Clean end card with exact AA mark and website. | “Design for the first useful action. AA Growth Studio.” |

**Recording note:** If a product screen is used, it must be a real product screen with permission to show it. Otherwise keep the entire Reel as a paper/whiteboard explainer. This is a script only, not a published Reel.
