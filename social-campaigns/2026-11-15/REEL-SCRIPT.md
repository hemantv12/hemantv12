# Future Reel recording script — 15 November 2026

**Status:** Script only. No Reel was published.
**Target duration:** 20–25 seconds.
**Audience:** US ecommerce / DTC founders running Meta Ads.
**Primary keyword:** Meta Ads landing page design.

## Script and shot list
**00:00–00:03 — Hook**
Voiceover: “Your ad got the click. Does the landing page keep the promise?”
Shot: Tight crop of the original campaign artwork; animate the cobalt line from “Ad Promise” toward “Page Experience.”

**00:03–00:08 — The problem**
Voiceover: “A visitor clicks for one benefit, then lands on a page that starts a different conversation.”
Shot: Use a real, permissioned screenshot of the brand's own ad and destination page side by side. Do not fabricate UI or use another company's creative.

**00:08–00:16 — Three checks**
Voiceover: “Match the promise. Keep the offer visible. Continue the same next step.”
Shot: Three clean text overlays—PROMISE / OFFER / NEXT STEP—over actual approved campaign/page material.

**00:16–00:21 — Practical close**
Voiceover: “If people have to reconnect the dots, you've added a detour.”
Shot: Return to the continuous-line graphic; bridge the visual gap.

**00:21–00:25 — CTA**
Voiceover: “Connect your paid creative to a clearer landing page with AA Growth Studio.”
Shot: Official logo and aagrowthstudio.com.

## Production requirements
- Record original voiceover or use an approved human voice asset.
- Use only the user's own ad creative and landing page screenshots, with permission to publish.
- No synthetic customers, fake analytics, fabricated campaign results or unlicensed footage.
- Export MP4/MOV and validate duration, resolution, audio and ownership before any Reel publishing action.
