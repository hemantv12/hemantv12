## Instagram caption
Your Meta ad gets the click. The landing page has to keep the promise.

For US ecommerce teams, check the handoff before rewriting another ad:

• Promise — does the first headline echo the benefit the ad led with?
• Offer — is the same product, bundle or price easy to spot?
• Next step — does the page continue the action the ad invited?

If the visitor has to reconnect the story, the experience has added a detour.

## Facebook native photo caption
Someone clicks your Meta ad because something in it feels relevant. The first screen of the landing page should confirm that decision.

For an ecommerce campaign, compare three things side by side:

- The promise: does the page headline continue the ad's main benefit?
- The offer: is the product, bundle or price visible without hunting?
- The next step: does the page make the same action clear?

A stronger ad-to-landing-page handoff is not about repeating every word. It is about making the next step feel like a natural continuation.
