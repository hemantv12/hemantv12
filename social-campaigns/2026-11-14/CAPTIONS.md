## Instagram caption
An empty screen still has a job.

For SaaS product teams, “No data yet” isn't a complete empty state. It leaves the user to work out what belongs here—and what to do next.

Design the state, not just the populated screen:
• First use: explain what belongs here and offer one clear create or import action.
• No results: echo the search or filter and make the reset obvious.
• Completed: acknowledge progress and point to a useful next step.
• Error: explain what failed and offer a safe recovery path. Don't disguise it as an empty state.

One screen. One context. One relevant next move.

## Facebook native photo caption
Your SaaS product has more than one kind of empty screen. They shouldn't all say “No data yet.”

A first-use state should explain what belongs there and show one clear create or import action. A no-results state should make the search or filter clear and offer a reset. A completed state can acknowledge progress. An error state should explain what failed and offer a safe way to recover.

Before shipping a generic placeholder, ask:
• What state is the user actually in?
• What should they understand?
• What is the one next action that fits this moment?

Design the state—not just the populated screen.
