## Instagram caption
Ecommerce checkout optimization starts with the questions a shopper shouldn't have to chase down.

For a US DTC store, review the path from cart to confirmation:
• Show the full order total—shipping, tax and fees—before the final step.
• Make guest checkout easy to find.
• State a realistic delivery estimate and surface the return policy near the decision point.
• Show clear payment choices and explain errors in plain language.

Then test the flow in a fresh session, as a first-time buyer. Note every surprise, detour and unclear message. Fix the most obvious point of hesitation first; don't redesign the whole checkout on instinct.

## Research note
Baymard Institute's 3 November 2026 checkout benchmark reports that 59% of desktop and mobile sites scored mediocre or worse, and notes that 17% of surveyed shoppers abandoned an online purchase in the prior three months because checkout was too long or complex.
