## Campaign: Don't Demo the Menu. Demo the Moment.

### Instagram carousel caption
A B2B SaaS product demo should make one workflow feel real—not parade every feature.

If you're a SaaS founder or product lead, build the walkthrough around the job your buyer came to solve:

• Before: understand their current process and where it slows down.
• Path: show one honest trigger → action → result.
• Fit: pause and ask how that would work in their environment.
• Proof: agree on one task, one owner, one date and one success signal for the next step.

Skip the feature parade. Give the buyer a clear way to judge fit.
