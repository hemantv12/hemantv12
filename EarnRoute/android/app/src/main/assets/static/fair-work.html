Safety and fair-work principles

EarnRoute is designed to make the details of paid short-term assignments easier to inspect before a worker accepts an offer. These principles do not replace applicable employment law, workplace safeguards or a participant's judgment.

Before you accept

  • Review the exact workplace, date, start and end times and the named business.
  • Check the gross compensation, pay basis, payment timing, platform charges and any agreed expenses.
  • Understand the responsibilities, eligibility requirements, dress code, equipment, breaks and safety instructions.
  • Keep the assignment confirmation and communicate changes through the platform where possible.

Personal safety

  • Do not travel to a location that materially differs from the published assignment without confirming it through a trusted channel.
  • Do not share OTPs, passwords, bank login details or unnecessary identity documents.
  • Report suspected scams, inappropriate behaviour, unsafe requests or pressure to pay unexpected fees.
  • Use workplace check-in methods only when you understand their purpose and the data being collected.

For businesses

  • Publish real work requirements and use a contact who can answer assignment questions.
  • State compensation and payment timing clearly before offers are accepted.
  • Do not discriminate on protected grounds or ask for irrelevant sensitive information.
  • Document material changes and resolve attendance, completion and payment issues promptly.

Honest limits

Platform business verification is not a government endorsement, background check, insurance guarantee or certification that a workplace is safe. A match score is a ranking aid, not an employment or earnings promise. Support and moderation may require human review.
