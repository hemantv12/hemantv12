Terms and conditions

Starter draft · Requires business and legal review before public launch.

EarnRoute is a marketplace for discovering and coordinating work opportunities. It does not guarantee employment, shift availability, income, workplace conditions or payment by another party.

1. Accounts and eligibility

Workers must be at least 18 years old for the initial service. You must provide accurate information, protect your sign-in credentials, and notify support when you suspect unauthorized access. Business accounts must provide truthful details and complete the platform's verification workflow before publishing opportunities.

2. Job listings and applications

Businesses are responsible for posting genuine opportunities and disclosing material details, including responsibilities, place and timing of work, gross compensation, applicable platform charges, payment terms, safety instructions, eligibility and cancellation conditions. Workers decide whether to apply and accept offers. An application is not a promise of employment; only a confirmed assignment reflects acceptance recorded by the platform.

3. Compensation and fees

Amounts displayed by EarnRoute are based on information recorded for the relevant listing or assignment. Gross pay, platform fees, net payable amounts and completed payouts are separate. A pending status does not mean that money has been paid. Any subscription or service fee must be disclosed before it is charged. No card or bank credentials should be sent through chat or uploaded into profile fields.

4. Attendance, completion and disputes

Workers and businesses must follow the published assignment instructions and applicable workplace rules. A recorded check-in or check-out may need additional verification. Participants may raise a dispute through the platform. EarnRoute may investigate or moderate the account or listing according to a published process, but does not promise that every dispute can be resolved in a particular user's favour.

5. Cancellations and conduct

Parties should communicate cancellations promptly and comply with the disclosed cancellation policy. Threats, harassment, discriminatory listings, fraudulent opportunities, unsafe requests and attempts to bypass disclosed charges may be reported for review. Do not pay a fee to a person who promises guaranteed work or earnings.

6. Privacy and service limitations

Personal data should be used only for account operation, relevant applications, assignments, support, fraud prevention and legally required activities. Location access, when introduced, must be explicit and limited to a stated purpose. Feature availability depends on the actual integrations enabled for the account and region.

7. Changes and contact

These terms are a product draft and must be updated to reflect the actual operator, legal entity, services, fees, settlement model, complaint process and applicable law before accepting public users. Replace support@earnroute.example with a monitored address before launch.
