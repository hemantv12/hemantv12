Privacy notice

Working draft v1.1 · Updated 19 October 2026 · Requires the responsible operating entity, monitored contact and legal review before public launch.

EarnRoute should collect only data needed to operate the work marketplace. Do not store Aadhaar numbers, card details or bank-login credentials in this app.

Information in an account

The initial application stores an email, display name, password hash, role, work preferences, relevant profile details, job records, applications, assignments, notifications and audit events. Businesses may store a business name, category, city and contact information. Do not add sensitive identity documents until a private storage, access-control, retention and deletion process is implemented.

How information is used

Information is used to display relevant opportunities, support applications and assignments, notify participants of recorded status changes, prevent abuse, review disputes and operate the service. Profile information is disclosed to a business only where needed to consider the worker's application. Admin access should be limited to legitimate operational purposes and recorded for sensitive actions.

Location and matching

The initial matcher uses supplied skills, availability, city, compensation preferences, experience requirements and recorded schedule conflicts. It does not use continuous background location tracking. Nearby search may use device GPS or a point the user selects manually on the map. The selected search point is sent to EarnRoute for matching and kept in temporary device session storage for up to 30 minutes; it is not added to the worker profile, and the app does not continuously track workers in the background. Published workplace coordinates are supplied by the business. Directions may open a third-party map app, and displayed distances are straight-line estimates rather than road distances or ETAs.

Security and retention

Passwords are hashed, and private API routes require server-verified role permissions. Production deployment also requires a unique secret, HTTPS, a production database, backups, suitable rate limiting, monitoring and reviewed access controls. Users can download a JSON copy of their account data from Profile → Privacy & account. The export excludes password hashes and session tokens, and lists sections capped at 500 records (support tickets are capped at 100). Users can submit access-help, correction or deletion requests from the same screen. These requests create private support tickets for review; they do not automatically delete an account or records. The operator must assess active assignments, disputes, security/audit needs and applicable retention obligations before acting on a deletion request.

Third-party services

Email, maps and payments remain optional until correctly configured. When enabled, their providers may process data under their own terms. The operator must identify these providers and update this notice accordingly.

Your choices and contact

New registrations record the exact draft Terms and Privacy notice revision IDs accepted; older accounts are not retroactively assigned a revision they never accepted. This is a product-control improvement, not a declaration of legal compliance. Before launch, name the responsible operating entity, replace support@earnroute.example with a monitored contact, publish a retention schedule and complaint-response procedure, and obtain Indian privacy/legal review of this notice and the actual service.
