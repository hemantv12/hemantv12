01Shopify app · SaaS

AdStockGuard

Stop paying for ads on sold-out products.

A Shopify app that pauses Meta, Google, TikTok and Pinterest ads the moment a product sells out and turns them back on when stock returns, with sales-velocity forecasting to warn merchants before they run out.

  • Ads paused within seconds of a stock-out, resumed on restock
  • Inventory webhooks from Shopify, no developer needed
  • Stock-out forecasting from weighted sales velocity
  • Savings dashboard by product, campaign and period
Live
  • Shopify App
  • Webhooks
  • Meta · Google · TikTok · Pinterest Ads APIs
  • Web dashboard

02Android · Web platform

EarnRoute

Find paid work close to home.

A hyperlocal earning marketplace built for India. Workers find genuine paid shifts within 1 to 15 km, businesses post and staff them, and admins verify every listing before it goes live.

  • Nearby shifts sorted by distance and take-home pay
  • Worker, business and admin workspaces
  • Interactive map with workplace pins
  • English and Hindi throughout
Launch candidate
  • Python · FastAPI
  • PostgreSQL
  • Android
  • Leaflet + OpenStreetMap
  • PWA

06IoT · Cloud · Mobile app

Connected 4G Dash Camera

See your car from anywhere.

A cellular-connected dash camera platform for the UK and North American markets. Live 1080p view from anywhere, real-time vehicle location and instant impact alerts while parked, with the device, cloud and app designed together.

  • Live front and rear view over 4G, even behind carrier NAT
  • GPS location reported while driving and parked
  • Impact and motion alerts within seconds, with the event clip
  • Signed over-the-air firmware updates with automatic rollback
In development
  • 4G LTE · GNSS
  • MQTT over TLS
  • WebRTC
  • eSIM (eUICC)
  • Mobile apps
